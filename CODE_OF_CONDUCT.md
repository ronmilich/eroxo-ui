# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in Eroxo UI issues, reviews and discussions deserves respectful treatment. Critique the work, give specific feedback and make room for differing experience and perspectives.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual attention and disclosure of private information are grounds for moderation. Maintainers may remove content, close a conversation or restrict participation.

For help moderating a project conversation, tag the repository owner, @ronmilich. To report harassment privately to GitHub, use **Report abuse** on the content or account. Include a link when available and keep personal details out of public issues.
